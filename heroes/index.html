    How the ranking works

    Each hero adds its star stats to attack and defence for its own troop type, its widget adds lethality and health for that type, and every Expedition skill counts at its level (the stars cap it). The widget skill counts only in its own role: a rally widget in a rally, a defender widget in a garrison.

    Bear: the expected damage of the lineup at its own ideal troop mix, with every chance skill at its average. The bar is each lineup against your best one.

    Rally and garrison: your lineup fights each stock opponent of your kingdom’s generation, both sides at 100,000 troops in the usual mixes, 24 battles a fight. Lineups are sorted by how many more troops they keep than the opponent, on average.

    Joiners add their first skills by the joiner rule. City buffs and pets are each their own layer that multiplies the stat, and an enemy debuff only cancels a buff in the same layer. Against the Bear only your attack and lethality buffs count.

    Not counted: appointments, turrets and lineups with two heroes of one troop type. The stock opponents, with their joiners, pets and city buffs, are estimates around the KingShot Battle Helper’s benchmark accounts, not measured players.

    Choose a role

    Understand Castle Battle combat, joiners and troop choices

    Role labels are a shortlist. Your stars, skills and alliance plan make the final call.

    Garrison lead

    Defending is a full-kit job. Start with a developed defensive lineup and a defender widget. A stronger reinforcement can change whose stats the garrison uses; check the active leader.

    Joining & reinforcing

    Slot 1 is the important one. Its first Expedition skill can take one of four shared joiner slots. Send the hero and skill level your alliance asks for; slots 2 and 3 do not add joiner skills.

    Offensive rally lead

    The caller brings the account stats and all three heroes’ Expedition kits. A rally widget helps, but compare the lineup you have built. A good caller is not automatically a good slot-1 joiner.

    Bear Hunt

    The Bear rewards damage. A caller uses the full kit; a joiner brings troops and a useful first skill. Defensive bonuses do not add Bear damage. Follow your alliance’s joiner list and duplicate rules.
