Choose a role

Garrison lead

The defensive stat source supplies the developed hero kit and applicable account bonuses. Compare defensive widgets and battle reports. A stronger reinforcing account can change the stat source; check the active garrison.

Joining & reinforcing

Put the assigned hero in slot 1. Its first Expedition skill can enter one of four shared joiner slots. Match offensive or defensive effects to the objective and coordinate skill levels. The other two heroes do not supply extra joiner skills.

Offensive rally lead

The caller provides account stats and the full three-hero Expedition kit. Rally widget skills are a useful starting point; compare the actual developed lineup and opponent. A lead recommendation does not automatically make a good slot-1 joiner.

Bear Hunt

The Bear is a damage objective. Leaders use their full kit; joiners supply troops and a qualifying first skill. Defensive effects do not improve Bear damage. Follow the alliance's allowed joiner list, especially for conditional or duplicate-sensitive choices.
