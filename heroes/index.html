Choose a role

Role labels are a shortlist. Your stars, skills and alliance plan make the final call.

Garrison lead

Defending is a full-kit job. Start with a developed defensive lineup and a defender widget. A stronger reinforcement can change whose stats the garrison uses; check the active leader.

Joining & reinforcing

Slot 1 is the important one. Its first Expedition skill can take one of four shared joiner slots. Send the hero and skill level your alliance asks for; slots 2 and 3 do not add joiner skills.

Offensive rally lead

The caller brings the account stats and all three heroes’ Expedition kits. A rally widget helps, but compare the lineup you have built. A good caller is not automatically a good slot-1 joiner.

Bear Hunt

The Bear rewards damage. A caller uses the full kit; a joiner brings troops and a useful first skill. Defensive bonuses do not add Bear damage. Follow your alliance’s joiner list and duplicate rules.
