    How the ranking works

    A hero’s stars add attack and defence for its own troop type. Its widget and the gear you saved on the Hero Gear page add lethality and health for that type, the same sums as the Expedition block of its Hero Stats. Every Expedition skill counts at its level, which the stars cap. Your Town Centre’s Truegold level, saved on the Buildings page, adds attack, defence, lethality and health to every march. The Bonus Overview leaves this out, and so far only TG2, TG4 and TG5 are known. A widget skill only counts in its own job: a rally widget in a rally, a defender widget in a garrison.

    For the Bear, each lineup is scored by its expected damage at its own best troop mix, with every chance skill counted at its average. The bar shows how it compares with your best lineup.

    For rallies and garrisons, each lineup fights every stock opponent from your kingdom’s generation 24 times, with both sides at 100,000 troops in the usual mixes. Lineups are then sorted by how many more troops they keep than the opponent, on average.

    Joiners add their first skills under the usual joiner rule. City buffs and pets each multiply the stat as a separate layer, and an enemy debuff only cancels a buff in its own layer. Against the Bear, only your attack and lethality buffs count.

    This leaves out appointments, turrets and lineups with two heroes of the same troop type. The stock opponents, with their joiners, pets and city buffs, are estimates based on the KingShot Battle Helper’s benchmark accounts, not real players.

    Choose a role

    Garrison lead

    Defending uses the whole kit, so start with a well-built defensive lineup and a defender widget. A stronger reinforcement can end up leading the garrison, so check whose stats it’s using.

    Joining & reinforcing

    Only slot 1 matters when you join. That hero’s first Expedition skill can fill one of the rally’s four shared joiner spots, and slots 2 and 3 add nothing. Send the hero and skill level your alliance asks for.

    Offensive rally lead

    The caller brings their own stats and all three heroes’ Expedition kits. A rally widget helps, but judge the lineup you’ve actually built, and remember a good caller isn’t always a good slot-1 joiner.

    Bear Hunt

    The Bear is all about damage, so defensive bonuses don’t help. A caller uses all three heroes’ kits, while a joiner brings troops and one useful first skill. Follow your alliance’s joiner list and its rules on duplicates.
