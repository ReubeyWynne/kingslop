Event Cycle

After your kingdom’s first KvK, events follow a 28-day cycle. Day 1 is Monday; this clock uses UTC.

Vikings

Vikings come at your alliance's cities in waves, and every Viking your troops kill is points — at your own city and at every city you reinforced.
