The XP costs are the same for every player on every server. Your rate is yours: free VIP XP is roughly 200–500 a day depending on logins, the Alliance Store and events, so your dates change with your rate.

The milestones

Three levels worth aiming for, with your date for each:

VIP 4 Construction speed +10%

The first level where activating VIP is generally worth it.

VIP 6 An extra march queue

The perk most players notice first: an extra march means more gathering and more rallies.

VIP 9 Construction +20% and combat stats

Construction +20% and the first combat stat boost. Past this level the costs double each time, so free players usually stop here.

Past VIP 9 the cost doubles each level: 600k, 1.2M, 2.4M. The page will work out those dates too, but the numbers get large quickly.

How the dates are worked out:

The rate is earned ÷ days: everything your progress bar has banked, divided by every day you've played.

Each level's date is today + still-needed ÷ rate. Still-needed comes from the table; the rate comes from your own numbers.

The formula, in one line

rate = earned ÷ days · date = today + still-needed ÷ rate

Nothing here is estimated. The costs are the game's fixed table, the progress is what the game shows, and days played is your own count. The division just divides.
