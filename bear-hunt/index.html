BEAR HUNT

How Bear damage works, and how to organise rallies around it.

30 min · every 48 hours · 5-minute rally cycle

The plan

The rally lead supplies the combat stats and full hero kit. Joiners supply troop counts and tiers. Up to four joiner first-hero skills are shared across the rally.

  • Launch enough rallies to keep returning join queues busy; do not restrict launches to only the strongest leads.
  • Use a join-march cap that fits your alliance's current rally capacity. Ours is about 85k right now, which usually leaves room for 13–14 joiners.
  • Keep your join marches as equal as practical. Divide archers evenly between the join queues, then fill the remaining space with cavalry and infantry, weighted more toward cavalry.
  • Use a recommended first hero on join marches, or send no hero.

A stronger lead raises the damage of every march in that rally. An extra rally gives returning join queues another place to score. If the same march can hit both, there is no need to choose between them.

For each troop type, damage grows roughly with the square root of the number you send. Doubling the troops in one march gives about 41% more damage, not 100%.

Exact model

For one participant, a useful proportional form is:

D ∝ KI√I + KC√C + KA√A

The K values include the rally lead's attack and lethality, troop base attack, hero and widget effects, and the Bear matchup. Chance-based skills vary across the ten rounds, so the planning model uses their average effect.

Aim for 13–14 joiners in each rally. The join-march cap should be set for your alliance's current progression and rally capacities.

For our alliance that cap is currently about 85k troops. It is not a universal Bear number, and we should expect to change it as rally capacities grow.

The cap leaves room for more players. An oversized join march can use enough rally capacity to keep another player out, losing a separate damage calculation.

For a designated strong lead, the join cap does not apply to their own lead march. Fill that march and use the lead's tested or simulated ratio. Their join marches into other rallies still use the alliance join cap.

Why 13–14?

If the same useful troops are spread over 14 participants instead of 10, the idealised square-root gain is about 18%. Real rallies are not perfectly equal, but losing several participant slots still costs damage.

Keep the join marches equal. In a live hunt you cannot reliably choose which lead gets your stronger join march without slowing the whole flow, so there is little value in building one good join and several weaker ones.

1. Divide archers evenly

Split your available archers equally across every join queue. The allocator below can also include your own rally; if your own rally is unusually strong, reserve its archers first and then divide the remainder equally between the join queues.

2. Fill with cavalry and infantry

Top each join march up to your alliance's cap with cavalry and infantry. Cavalry should be weighted higher than infantry for Bear. We do not have enough evidence to pretend there is one exact cavalry/infantry split that is correct for every account and lead.

5 / 15 / 80 is a rough long-term troop shape, not a per-march routing trick. As your troop pool grows, all of your equal join marches can become more archer-heavy together. At our current 85k cap, six full join marches contain 510k troops; a literal 5/15/80 split across all six would require about 25.5k infantry, 76.5k cavalry and 408k archers. Until you have that troop depth, spread what you have evenly rather than forcing one march to the target ratio.

The allocator only divides archers, and every join queue always gets the same archer count. Fill the rest of each join march with cavalry and infantry, using more cavalry than infantry. The 80% archer ceiling is a rough late-progression target, not an instruction to make some joins better than others.

Use two groups for the opening rallies. After those are launched, relaunch as soon as your rally heroes return.

  1. Poll before the event. Ask who is available and whether they prefer Group 1 or Group 2. Allow for people who do not answer and for late arrivals.
  2. Group 1 launches immediately. Choose enough starters to use most of the join queues available at the start. A small surplus is fine if one or two leads are willing to cancel.
  3. Group 2 launches at roughly 28:00–27:30 remaining. Group 1 join marches should be home in time to join them.
  4. If Group 1 does not fill, cancel a flex rally. Relaunch it with Group 2 instead of letting it fire half-empty.
  5. After that, relaunch on return. Joiners take the next suitable rally that is close to firing. Travel time and normal player timing will spread the later launches.

Fast relaunch: while your lead march is walking home, open the rally screen and tap your saved Bear formation. It will not load while the troops are away; the first tap after they arrive loads it immediately.

How many rallies in Group 1?

Estimate the join queues available at the start and divide by the number of joiners you want per rally. With 15 players and six join queues each, 90 opening join queues support about seven rallies with 13 joiners. Starting eight can still work if one or two leads are ready to cancel when needed.

After the first hits, returning join queues can be used again, so later rallies are not limited by the opening number.

On a join march, only the first hero can contribute to the rally's four shared joiner skills. Use one of the recommended first heroes below; if none are available, send the march with no hero.

A random first hero can occupy one of the four shared slots with a defensive or otherwise unhelpful skill. If your hero is selected, that skill applies to the whole rally rather than only to your march.

Recommended joiner first heroes

Gen I: Chenko, Yeonwoo, Amane; Amadeus when he is not needed as your lead.

Gen II: no new default joiner hero. Hilde is alliance-policy dependent; many alliances do not allow her, so follow your alliance's rule.

Gen IV: Margot, Rosa.

Gen V: Vivian.

Gen VI: Yang.

Later options: Petra (avoid duplicate Petras in one rally), Ava, Wee & Woo.
