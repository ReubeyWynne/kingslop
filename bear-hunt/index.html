BEAR HUNT

How Bear damage works and how to organise rallies around it.

30 min · every 48 hours · 5-minute rallies

Your hunt

  1. 1

    Everyone rallies, however weak.

    Weak players still take up spaces in other rallies, so they need to launch their own to give those spaces back. A weak rally can launch with just one troop.

  2. 2

    Fill every rally to 15.

    That's everyone else, since a player joins each rally only once. Join whichever rally is closest to marching out, and relaunch yours as soon as it's home.

  3. 3

    With 14 rallies to join, 6 joins reach every one of them every five minutes, so make them bigger rather than send six.

The plan

The rally leader's stats and heroes set the strength of the rally. Each player's infantry, cavalry and archers are then scored separately, and up to four joiners' first heroes add their skills to the whole rally.

  • Everyone should launch a rally every five minutes. Spread the launches out so each rally has time to fill.
  • Fill every rally to 14 joiners. The real limit in Bear is spaces in rallies.
  • Cap joins at 85,000 so every rally fills. The cap goes up as rally capacity grows.
  • Keep your joins the same size. Split archers evenly first, then cavalry, then fill the rest with infantry. For full joins, 5 / 15 / 80 is a good ratio.
  • Join with one of the recommended first heroes. If you don't have one, join with no hero.

A strong rally leader still matters, because it makes every hit in that rally stronger. Everything else here is about having enough rallies open that everyone's marches keep hitting the Bear.

Each troop type's damage grows with roughly the square root of how many you send. Send twice the archers and you get about 41% more archer damage, not double.

Each march in a rally is scored on its own, and its infantry, cavalry and archers each get their own square root. So the same troops do more damage spread across several joins than packed into one.

Exact model

For one march in a rally:

D ∝ KI√I + KC√C + KA√A

Each K covers the rally leader's attack and lethality, the troop's base attack, hero and widget bonuses, and the Bear matchup. Some skills only trigger by chance during the ten rounds, so the model uses their average.

Aim for 14 joiners in every rally. Full rallies give returning marches the most places to join.

If everyone launches a rally every five minutes and sends 6 joins, full rallies let each join hit the Bear up to 2.33 times every five minutes. Empty spaces, or players who don't launch, bring that down.

The joiner cap is 85,000 troops, your biggest rally ÷ 16. That's your alliance's number, not a Bear rule, and it goes up as rally capacity grows. It stops a few big joins using up a rally's capacity and leaving spaces empty.

The cap doesn't apply to a strong leader's own rally. Fill it with whatever ratio works for that leader. Their joins into other rallies still follow the cap.

A weak rally can launch with one troop. It still opens 14 spaces for everyone else, and all of that player's troops go to stronger rallies.

Why 14, and why everyone launches

Say P players have six marches each, L of them launch every five minutes, and each rally has S joiners on average. Each march then hits H = L × S ÷ (P × 6) times every five minutes.

That's highest when L = P because everyone launches, and S = 14 because every rally fills. That gives H = 14 ÷ 6 = 2.33. With 13 joiners on average it drops to 2.17; with 14 joiners but only 80% of players launching, it's 1.87.

The 14th joiner adds damage to that rally, but the bigger gain is that returning marches reuse that space all event.

With fewer than 15 players, S can't go past P − 1, because a player joins each rally only once. Every five minutes, each player has P − 1 rallies to join, however many marches they send. About (P − 1) ÷ 2.33 joins reach all of them, so the rest of your troops can make those joins bigger, up to the cap. Damage grows with the square root of a join's troops, so fewer, bigger joins do more.

Keep all your joins the same size. Split archers evenly first, then cavalry, then fill the rest with infantry. Once you have the troops to fill every join, 5 / 15 / 80 is the ratio we use.

1. Equal marches first

Don't make one great join and five weak ones. You can't choose which rally each march ends up in without slowing everyone down, so keep them even. After archers, use cavalry before infantry.

2. Use 5 / 15 / 80 when full

For the rally leaders we've tested, 5% infantry / 15% cavalry / 80% archers comes very close to the best possible ratio. The exact ratio depends on the leader. At a cap of 85,000, that's about 4,250 infantry, 12,750 cavalry and 68,000 archers per join.

6 full joins of 85,000 at 5 / 15 / 80 need about 25,500 infantry, 76,500 cavalry and 408,000 archers in total. Until you have that many, keeping your joins equal matters more than hitting the ratio.

Everyone should launch a rally every five minutes, just not all at once. Start with two groups of rallies, then relaunch as soon as your rally comes home.

  1. Poll before the event. Ask who's coming and whether they want the first or second group. Leave room for people who don't answer and people who turn up late.
  2. The first group launches straight away. Open enough rallies that most people have somewhere to join. One or two extra is fine if those leaders don't mind cancelling.
  3. The second group launches between 29:00 and 28:30. Those rallies should still be waiting when the first group's joins get home. Leave enough time to hit the Bear, go home and join the next rally.
  4. If the first group doesn't fill, cancel a rally. Relaunch it with the second group instead of letting it go half-empty.
  5. After that, everyone relaunches as soon as they're home. Join whichever rally is closest to marching out. Travel times spread the later rallies out on their own.

Fast relaunch: while your rally is marching home, open the rally screen and tap your saved Bear formation. It won't load until those troops are back, so tap it again the moment they arrive.

How many rallies in the first group?

Count the joins free at the start and divide by 14. 15 players with 6 joins each is 90, enough for 6 full rallies. One more rally can work too, if its leader is happy to cancel when fewer people turn up.

Once the first hits land, those marches come home and join again, so the size of the first group doesn't limit the rest of the event.

When you join, only your first hero counts. The first-hero skills of up to four joiners apply to the whole rally, so use one of the heroes below. If you don't have any of them, join with no hero.

Any other first hero can take one of those four spots with a defensive or useless skill. If it gets picked, it affects the whole rally, not just your march.

First heroes for joining

Gen I: Chenko, Yeonwoo, Amane, and Amadeus when you aren't leading with him.

Gen II–III: nothing new for joining. Hilde depends on your alliance; many ban her, so check your alliance's rule.

Gen IV: Margot, Rosa.

Gen V: Vivian.

Gen VI: Yang.

Later options: Petra (only one per rally), Ava, Wee & Woo.
