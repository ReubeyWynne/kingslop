Upgrade priorities

Points follow these priorities. Lead buildings climb together, lowest first. Keep up waits for Lead until it falls a whole TG tier behind the Town Centre. Spare gets only what is left. Skip never upgrades for its own sake. A building's prerequisites, like the Town Centre and Embassy, are upgraded first whatever their priority. Ties go to the building higher in the list.

            How points are counted

            Item rates come from the Event Cycle guide. Speedups score per minute: general speedups count on any speedup day; healing counts only when all kinds are accepted.

            Upgrade items count only when they can fund a complete step. Heroes use their own shards and widgets, general shards and matching generation chests. Hero gear upgrades also need enough XP and spare gear. Truegold counts only what your buildings can take. Hero points breakdown

            Governor gear and charms use the score gain from upgrades your materials can fund, multiplied by the event rate.

            Truegold and Tempered Truegold count only the building upgrades they can fund, one whole step at a time, in the order your upgrade priorities set. Each step also needs its resources, its Town Centre level and its prerequisites. Unset buildings and uncounted resources add nothing. The points-only figure spends the cheapest Truegold step anywhere instead, which is the most your buildings can take. Costs stop at TG 8 until a second source confirms higher levels.

            Totals exclude training, gathering, intel, roulette and pet advancement. Missing counts are left out and mark the total as partial.
