        How the points are counted

        Items use the rates in the Event Cycle guide: each one is worth its points on the days it is listed. Speedups count one point rate per minute; general speedups count on any speedup day, and healing speedups only on days that take every kind.

        Governor gear and charms score by the governor score they add. The page spends your materials for the most score, using the same planner as the Governor gear page, then multiplies by the day's rate.

        Training, gathering, intel missions, roulette spins and pet advancement depend on what you do on the day, so they are listed but not counted. A count you have not entered is left out and marks the total as partial. Check unlocked tiers and requirements in game before you spend.
