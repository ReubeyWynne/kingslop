# Pets

The Pets page (`pets/`) has two tabs: **Your pets** for viewing and planning in-game Advances, and **Pet pen** for looking after the animals.

## Data

`_data/pets.json` is generated by `.dsh/build-pets.py` from the progression research (`research/progression/data/pets.json` in the project files). Do not edit it by hand. It holds:

- `pets`: id, name, generation, `unlockDay` (Kingshot Data's day for the generation, about), `maxLevel`, `profile`, `advances` (one per ten levels) and the skill: its name, the in-game description at the top level (`say`) and the ladder, one value per Advance;
- `profiles`: the five cost profiles every pet in a generation shares, each level with `petFood` (the price of reaching it), `advance` (the extra price of breaking its cap) and `score` (its Pet Advancement score), plus `totals` from level 1 to max;
- `points`: KvK / Strongest Governor and Brawl points per Advance (score × 50 and × 30).

The three sources agree on every row but three cells, where the two that agree win (`research/progression/pets.md`). Ironclad War Bear has no KS h5joy page, so it is `verified: false` and its dossier says so. The passive attack and defence bonus (one source) and refinement (the guides disagree on the tiers; no roll odds) are left out until they are checked in game.

## Advances

`js/pet-plan.mjs` (`PetPlan`) works from a pet's level and whether the Advance at that level is done. The skill level is the number of Advances done. `nextAdvance` is the cost of the next cap with its Advance, `toMax` the cost of every step left; both carry the Advances' score and points. An unread level counts as nothing.

## Care

`js/pet-care.mjs` (`PetCare`) keeps two meters per tamed pet, fullness and affection, as a value and the time it was set; the current value is worked out from the clock, so pets get hungry (empty over 16 hours) and lonely (over 10) whether or not the page is open. Pet Food adds 30, a pat 20, and a pet over 90 full turns food down. The mood follows the whole-point meters: asleep at night (23:00–06:00 local) after ten minutes alone, then hungry (below 25), lonely (below 25), delighted (both 70 or more), content (average 45 or more), else restless. The care lives under `ks.pet-care.v1` in local storage, not in the ledger, so it never reaches saves or the event maths; an unreadable record starts again.

### Familiarity

Care now also builds a permanent bond in the same local care record. An accepted meal or pat earns one care day on a new local date, at least twenty hours after the previous credit. Extra care still fills the meters and plays its reaction; missed days never remove bond progress. The stages are getting acquainted, familiar (3 days), close companion (7) and trusted friend (14). Existing care records start with no bond without losing their meters. This remains device-local play, separate from in-game pet levels, materials and ledger exports.

A familiar pet greets a returning player after four hours away. Only visible tamed pens count as visited; sleeping pets stay asleep. The welcome uses the existing perked ears and a short head tilt, with the baked happy frames as fallback. Visits and discoveries do not earn care days.

Each species likes its head, cheeks or chin stroked. In live pens these hit areas follow the projected face as it turns; baked pens use three vertical regions. Most of a completed stroke must cross one area to discover it. Its stronger reaction adds more hearts and, in live pens, a head lean. Keyboard users choose an area with Up/Down and stroke with Enter or Space.

## Page

- **Your pets** follows the Heroes and Masters roster pattern: one game-art profile per pet, grouped by generation, with skill, cost and level controls inside each dossier. The 14 WebP profiles are cropped from the Kingshot 1.14.27 pet-avatar atlas: `python .dsh/pet-portrait/profiles.py path/to/pet-avatar-atlas.png` (NumPy, Pillow, SciPy). The roster and dossiers remain readable without JavaScript.
- **Pet pen** opens on an orbitable clearing with all fourteen full-body models. The animals shift position, look around and breathe at independent rhythms; the stream moves behind them. Selecting one zooms into a visit with only that animal. **Back to habitat** restores the picker.
- `js/pages/pet-den.mjs` owns the tabs and selected animal (`petDen`), the ledger-backed dossier (`pet`), local care (`petCare`), and event scores (`petAdvances`).
- During a visit, feed changes fullness, pet changes affection, and play rolls a toy for the animal to chase and builds affection. Moods and bonds appear beside its name. The text-only **Your pets** list opens the same care controls without WebGL. Up/Down on the Pet button changes where you stroke. Untamed pets react without writing care records; care never changes the ledger.
- Every dossier has `data-deep-link="pet-{id}"`, so `#pet-lion` opens its planning profile. It shows the next Advance before the detailed skill ladder. The collection's KvK total remains visible when the points breakdown is closed.
- The WebGL 2 world loads when the pen comes into view. Reduced motion keeps the full 3D scene and disables idle motion. Without WebGL, the text-only care controls remain available.
- All 14 pens use Kingshot 1.14.27 game meshes and diffuse maps. The APK identifies the DLC dependencies; the mesh and texture bytes come from those downloaded bundles. Internal names do not reliably match the species: Cheetah is `ovibos_moschatus`, Grizzly Bear is `snow_leopard`, Lynx is `north_wolf`, and Ironclad War Bear is `monitor_lizard`. See `pet-recovered/INDEX.md` in the downloads workspace for the full mapping. Most bodies are from the exhibit bundles; Moose and Ironclad War Elephant use their pit-scene bodies.
- Regal White Lion (`mammoth`) and Ironclad War Elephant (`gigantopithecus`) use the original skin weights, collapsed onto six pen drivers: body, neck, head, both ears and nose or trunk. These GLBs do not contain animation clips. The pen still supplies poses, expressions and reactions. The Elephant's UVs and diffuse map were recovered separately from its bundle.
- `shade.py` renders meshes offline in Chromium. Each cell gets a glyph from its light and one of seven inks from its colour. `.dsh/build-ascii.py` converts the nine poses in `.dsh/pet-portraits/<pet>.json` into stepped CSS. `--exhibits` expects `pet-models` alongside `pet-rigs` and `pet-recovered` in the downloads workspace. The latter is produced by `recover_priority_pet_meshes.py`.

  ```sh
  npm install --no-save --package-lock=false three@0.169.0 meshoptimizer@0.22.0 playwright@1.49.1
  npx playwright install chromium
  python -m pip install numpy pillow
  python .dsh/pet-portrait/shade.py --exhibits path/to/pet-models
  python .dsh/build-ascii.py
  ```

  To bake just one exhibit, append its pet ID. `shade.py --live [pet ...]` rewrites live settings without rebaking the camera or mesh.

- `js/pet-habitat.js` draws the indexed `pets/models/<pet>.bin` meshes together, using vertex colours and neck anchors from the companion JSON. Terrain, stream, trees and rocks share the models' depth buffer. A separate colour-ID pass resolves taps to the frontmost animal. The scene reacts to `data-mood` and `data-react` and stops drawing offscreen.

### Live shader tuning

Run `npm run serve` from the site directory and open `/pets/?shader=1#pet-play` on the HTTP preview. The pen fetches model binaries and metadata from the same origin; opening the HTML directly with `file://` does not work.

The whole scene uses one ASCII grid. Lighting, scene saturation, brightness, gamma and haze apply to the rendered world. The panel below the scene controls rows and columns independently (210 columns × 242 rows by default), along with ramp, edge strength and opacity. Luminance drives the fill glyphs; a Sobel gradient of luminance and coverage drives directional edge glyphs. Only glyph strokes carry opacity. Settings save locally for this opt-in view. Reset restores the shipped look; Copy JSON exports the current preset.
