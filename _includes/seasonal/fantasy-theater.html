How this is calculated

A pause pays {{ event.pauseTokensPerAmulet }} tokens per amulet. The ascent chance climbs 10%, 30%, 60%, 80%, then 100% on the fifth try, and an ascent is 1, 2 or 3 floors (80%, 19.67%, 0.33%). The top of the Theater gives 1–5 Fanstars, 1.5 on average; five send the next round into the Fortress.

The tokens per amulet are the long-run average with the best claim on every floor, solved exactly rather than simulated. Fortress floors 2 to 7 are counted at the Theater's rewards, since only floor 8's is published. KvK points are the prep values on the Event Cycle.
