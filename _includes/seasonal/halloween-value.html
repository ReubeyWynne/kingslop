How this is calculated

Attacks cost 20 candy and give 2 Prankster Points. Each Pumpkin hit, Pumpkin defeat and Witch hit has its own reward pool. The model follows those targets and the free-attack pity counter, then applies each chest’s exact drop rates to the expected chest counts.

The results average seeded runs. Small fractions mean a rare reward’s expected value. The shared jackpot is excluded. Candy-rain income uses the source’s 100-candy average.
