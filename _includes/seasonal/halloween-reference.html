Coven Clash and free attacks

Each attack costs {{ event.rules.attackCandy }} candy and earns {{ event.rules.attackPoints }} points. A Pumpkin Man takes {{ event.rules.pumpkinHits }} hits. Each kill has a 20% chance to summon the Witch, who stays for 3–15 hits. Auto Attack unlocks after 60 attacks.

Prankster Time starts at a 0.2% chance per paid attack through attack 12. Each subsequent paid attack adds 3.6 percentage points; attack 40 is certain. Free attacks are assumed not to advance this counter.

Witch’s Treasure

A Witch visit lasting at least 10 hits can roll for the server’s shared pot. The chance is 0% below 10,000 points, 0.6% from 10,000 and 1% from 20,000. The pot resets to 10,000 points and pays 0.4 candy per pot point. The source adds 2 pot points per kill.

The jackpot is excluded from forecasts because its current size and other players’ wins are unknown.

Candy income and Trick or Treat

1,000 gems spent gives 10 candy. Two hours of speedups used gives 10 candy. Gathering gives 10–20 candy per hour, capped at 3,000. Beasts give 10–30 each, capped at 4,000.

Trick or Treat runs for seven days in the source: one own-door visit and two shared-door visits per day. Each visitor to your shared house gives 20 candy.
