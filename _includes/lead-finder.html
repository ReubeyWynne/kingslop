        How the ranking works

        Your Bonus Overview is your account before any hero is seated, which is why a battle report won’t do. Each hero then adds its stars, widget, gear and Expedition skills, and your Truegold adds to every march. A widget skill only counts in its own job.

        For the Bear, a lineup scores its expected damage at its best troop mix, with chance skills at their average.

        For rallies and garrisons, a lineup fights each stock opponent 24 times, and lineups rank by how many more troops they keep.

        City buffs and pets multiply as separate layers. Against the Bear, only attack and lethality buffs count.

        Appointments, turrets and lineups with two heroes of one troop type aren’t counted. The stock opponents are estimates from the KingShot Battle Helper’s benchmarks, not real players.
