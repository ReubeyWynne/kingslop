    what can I screenshot?

    • Resources and speedups: both tabs of the Overview (the chart beside Backpack).
    • Pet levels: Pet List, on the Pets screen.
    • Hero gear: a hero’s gear screen.
    • The battle simulator: a battle report’s Battle Details.
