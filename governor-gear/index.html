The maths

Each row is what one more star or charm level costs. The planner takes one step at a time: the affordable upgrade with the most gain for the share of your bag it uses. It is a guide, not a guaranteed best order. Gear and charms use different materials, so they never compete.

Gear set bonuses are left out until a second table confirms them.
