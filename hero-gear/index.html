ranking & assumptions

at each larger budget, choose the red milestone with the highest weighted gain; ties favour gain per cost. these are independent choices, not three purchases in a queue. only expedition bonus milestones (+20, +60, +100) are compared; intervening upgrades are included in the cost.

weighted gain = added health or lethality × its profile weight + added attack × lethality weight + added defence × health weight. this is a priority score, not a battle-damage percentage. the % shown is weighted gain ÷ the weighted score of your current gear for the selected troops.

gain / cost = weighted gain ÷ Σ(resource cost ÷ reference amount). the same amounts apply to every choice: your budget or 52,650 XP / 550 hammers / 6 mythic / 10 mithril, whichever is larger per resource. these are comparison scales, not exchange prices. current reference amounts: .

future budgets increase every resource by the same fraction of those reference amounts. this makes comparable saving steps, not a prediction of how fast you collect resources. change your bag to rerank. role profiles are editable planning preferences; cavalry’s 65% catch-up reminder does not secretly change the score.

    how these KvK points score

    XP, XP reforge and sacrificed mythic gear add no KvK points. each saving choice shows the points for its full upgrade cost; the spend total covers all changed pieces, across every troop tab. these points score once, on the day you use the materials.

    what these costs include

    includes all missing mastery, ascension and intervening imbuements. each piece is compared against the same bag.

    after upgrading in game, mark upgrades done to save the levels and subtract the cost.

    how the best split works

    every piece's quality, red milestone and mastery fixes its hammer, spare mythic and mithril cost; every combination the set's total can pay for is checked, and leftover XP goes where it adds the most stat per XP. mastery, ascension and milestone choices are exact; levels can sit a level or two from the true best. the score uses your build profile weights, and conquest bonuses are outside it.

    hammers, mithril and spare mythic gear cannot be refunded in the game, so read this as a guide to where the next ones should go. XP can be reforged: Spend now covers that.

    the maths & sources

    XP, Forgehammers, spare mythic pieces and mithril are counted separately. milestone costs include missing mastery, its mythic cost, ascension and every intervening imbuement. costs start from your saved level; XP already partway to the next level is not counted.

    recommendations compare XP, mastery and bundled milestone paths over several passes, then keep the highest weighted result. XP reforge also compares against keeping your gear. the score covers expedition health, lethality, attack and defence; conquest bonuses are outside this score.
