the maths & sources

XP, Forgehammers, spare mythic pieces and mithril are counted separately. milestone costs include missing mastery, its mythic cost, ascension and every intervening imbuement. costs start from your saved level; XP already partway to the next level is not counted.

recommendations compare XP, mastery and bundled milestone paths over several passes, then keep the highest weighted result. XP reforge also compares against keeping your gear. the score covers expedition health, lethality, attack and defence; conquest bonuses are outside this score.
