TRY IT — YOUR BEAR RATIO

The lead's attack and lethality make their A factor per troop type. That alone fixes the mix the lead should run, and their strength K.

The ideal march lands here.

The exact mix depends on the lead's attack factors — not your stats or the march size. For ordinary join marches, the guide uses 5 / 15 / 80 because it has been very close to the exact optimum for the rally leaders we have tested; this panel shows the selected lead's exact optimum. Heroes are not in it yet — a type-specific skill would move this mix.

TRY IT — YOUR MARCH

The troops you send — not the rally's. Counts by type, and the tier each one marches at. The console compares your split with the lead's optimum for that same march.

Tier sets the scale, not the split: the three types' base attacks rise together, so one tier across the march cancels out. Only mixing tiers moves the optimum — and from T7+ / TG3+ archers pick up another ×1.1 against the bear.

Absolute damage scales with √(troops) of each type — double the march and you get ×1.41, not ×2. Every split rule holds at any scale, which is what lets the console answer before the constant is fitted.

Damage — and the bracket it lands in — is on the bench. Turning a split into a number needs one constant fitted from real reports: the bear's defence, the troops' base attack and the lead's hero skills all ride in it. The Frakinator's ladder doubles from 47M to 38.4B; until that constant is fitted, this panel shows the split, not a damage figure.

A ratio is the share of your march per type — infantry / cavalry / archers. The alt is the first fallback when the starting line still doesn't clear.

Five of the six rooms fix your troops at Level 10 — training more won't clear them; upgrading the system they check will. Only the Coliseum and the Radiant Spire let you pick your heroes.

Five attempts per room per day, refreshed at 00:00 UTC — and an attempt is only spent on a loss. Clear Stages 1–10 in a room and its Raid opens: +10 Trial Crystals a stage, every week.

Once the Trial Shop shows Truegold Dust, that's the buy — T11 gear wants it in volume.

Community tables put the Crystal Cave in the same Wed–Thu window as the Forest of Life; older notes list the pets alone. The event screen is the tiebreaker.

The clear predictor is on the bench. It needs per-stage enemy stats — readable from the right column of a trial report, the same way this page reads the left — before it can call a stage a clear. Until then: the room, the ratio, and what to upgrade.

PVE BEASTS — ON THE BENCH

World-map beasts and monsters, fought with your own army and heroes — no rally, no shared stats. The console is framed and waiting.

What already holds: your stats above, your march by type and tier, and the same √ rules that decide the bear. When the tables land, the answer has the same shape — what to send, and what it gets you.

What's missing: the enemy tables. Nothing here gets invented, so the panel carries no numbers until they're collected — from the right column of a beast report, the way this page reads a bear one.

How the console works it out:

Each troop type's A factor is its attack times its lethality: (1 + attack%) × (1 + lethality%). One factor per type, from the lead's report.

The damage weights are the troops' base attacks from the troop table — infantry ⅓, cavalry 1, archers 4.4/3 at T6, the archers' ×1.1 coming from the all-infantry bear. The ideal share is the weighted factor squared, normalised: ft ∝ (weightt·At)².

The formula, in one line

ft ∝ (Ainf/3, Acav, 4.4·Aarc/3)² · K = √(Σ wt²)

A march adds its damage up per type: √(troops) × base attack × A, summed over infantry, cavalry and archers. The absolute figure carries a constant for the bear's defence and army, plus the heroes' skills on top — that constant is the piece still missing.

Which leaves the march verdict a pure ratio: your split converts the cosine of what the same troops could do for this lead. The nearer your shares sit to ft, the nearer that number gets to 100%.

The march, in one line

D ∝ Σt √Nt·baset·At · split efficiency = Σ(btAt√ft) / √(Σ(btAt)²)

The Mystic Trial rooms are community tables (Kingshot Mastery), not extracted formulas — the ratios are a starting line, not a law. The bear maths is the Frakinator's.

The bear factor multiplies absolute damage but cancels out of every split — which is why the ratio holds at any scale. Hero skills are the exception: an all-troop buff cancels out too, a type-specific one moves the mix. More sims (heroes, the damage constant, PvP) are being researched; these are the first four.
