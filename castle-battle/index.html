Inspect your copy and first skill

{% endfor %}

Up to four member first skills contribute alongside the lead’s full kit. Slots 2 and 3 do not add their skills. A high-level hero with the wrong first skill does not do the requested job.

Selection and stacking are separate questions

A useful first skill must also be selected into the four slots. The official FAQ confirms the four skills, but does not explain every priority tie or duplicate interaction. Do not turn “eligible” into “four copies stack” without a controlled test.

Frontline time versus outgoing damage

Normal attacks hit the first surviving row. More infantry can keep the back rows attacking longer, but uses slots those damage troops could occupy. Cavalry bypass and hero effects complicate that tradeoff.

Why this is not a fixed Gen 3 ratio

Use troop tiers, actual per-type bonuses and the opponent’s surviving composition. Squad-wide hero buffs do not require a matching troop type, so the three hero types do not prescribe the troop split. The current simulator tests troop-only tradeoffs; it cannot establish an optimum for a hero lineup.

Give each rally a measurable job

Defend the interval between hits

Contribute to the intended hit or refill

Capture
Compare success rate and troops remaining to occupy, not just damage.
Depletion
Also measure how many active garrison troops each hit removes. A losing rally can leave a useful state for the next one.
Attrition
Compare enemy active troops removed per hit and your own troops removed. Rank losing formations by depletion and cost rather than assigning them all the same zero.
Follow-up
Re-evaluate the surviving infantry and total garrison. The next rally fights that state, plus any reinforcement, not the opening report again.
Cost
A large bonus-source gap can make capture unrealistic. Test whether the damage per hit justifies the troop cost before committing a wave.
Refills
Sum of damage across separate hits is not net depletion. Reinforcements between hits can restore the garrison and erase the progress.

Track the infantry deficit between arrivals and maintain the intended bonus source. Repeating an opening mixed refill can leave a depleted frontline even when capacity is full.

On attack, send the requested composition and first skill for the intended rally. On defense, send what the current garrison is missing; your own march ratio is only part of the combined army.

What a wave comparison must include

Arrival intervals, reinforcement counts and types, the active bonus source, heroes and selected joiner skills. Damage removed from active combat is not permanently killed troops; casualty categories and the next battle’s available troops must be handled separately.

Mechanics and evidence

The official Combat FAQ confirms row targeting, simultaneous turns, squad-wide skill activation without matching troops, independent hero effects and the rally/garrison skill sources. Hero effects and level values above come from the site’s shared hero data and linked official hero pages.

Lineup comparisons are inferences from those kits, not a tier list. The troop model is reverse engineered and omits heroes. No community ratio is used as a proven optimum.
