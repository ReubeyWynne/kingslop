Castle Battle

Take the castle, keep it reinforced, and hold it long enough to win.

Know your role

Agree the plan before battle. Know your assignment, join requirements and where calls will be made. Follow the live calls as targets and troop needs change.

Caller
Names targets, leads, troop requests and arrival times. Keeps everyone on the same plan.
Rally lead
Assigned attacker. Builds the rally kit and stats; launches on call.
Garrison lead
Assigned defender. Builds toward 5★ heroes, strong defender widgets and the core stats to hold.
Joiners
Meet the alliance's troop-tier, hero, skill-level and march-size requirements. Send what is called.
Build for your assignment

Agree your role before spending. Offensive and defensive leads may be different players; later generations give accounts more room to specialise. Match heroes, widgets, gear, charms and research to that role.

Keep designated leads available for their jobs. Prioritise the best available troop tiers among other players for the castle garrison. Those players can also fill attacking rallies when called.

Hold the castle

The King's Castle sits between four turrets. Your alliance wins on occupation time; personal battle points have their own rewards.

Prepare, take, hold, retake
Prepare
Assign castle and turret leads. Save rally and garrison presets; clear infirmary space.
Take
Scout. Fill the called rally. Have reinforcements ready for the capture.
Hold
Watch incoming attacks, active bonuses and remaining infantry. Refill the missing troops.
Retake
Scout again. The new occupant may have different heroes, bonuses and fresh troops.

Take the turrets too

A hostile turret fires on the castle garrison, faster as occupation continues. Your own team's turrets spare it. Taking turrets helps you break a hold and protect your own.

Local Castle Battle and KvK

Local Castle Battle is an alliance contest within your kingdom. KvK adds Kingdom of Power preparation and cross-kingdom rules. Check the KvK screen for access, ownership and turret bonuses.

How the fight runs

Both sides attack each turn. Losses apply before the next; the fight ends when one army is gone. Every surviving row attacks, including the troops behind your infantry.

Infantry
Take normal attacks first. Keep your damage troops alive.
Cavalry
Take over when infantry fall. Their troop skill can bypass to archers.
Archers
High damage; exposed last in normal targeting.

Counters apply to the troop type actually hit. Cavalry do not hit archers on every attack; hero skills can change targeting. Combat rules

Stats work in pairs

Deal damage
Attack × lethality
Survive it
Defense × health

Both pairs matter on attack and defense. Health keeps a rally fighting; lethality increases damage, rather than deciding permanent kills.

The maths

The Frakinator model uses these bonus factors:

Offense: base attack × (1 + ATK/100) × (1 + LET/100)
Resistance: base health × (1 + DEF/100) × (1 + HP/100)

At 500% ATK and 100% LET, the factors give 6 × 2 = 12. Another 100 points of ATK gives 14; the same added to LET gives 18. The lower paired stat can offer the bigger gain.

Damage also scales with the square root of each active troop count. Twice the archers gives roughly 41% more archer damage per turn. Extra troops also absorb more losses.

Check the skill

Squad-wide skills help the whole army, even without the hero's matching troop type. Read the skill's scope. Widget skills can require rally or defender duty.

Read a battle report
  1. Troops: starting counts, tiers and Truegold levels.
  2. Bonuses: whose stats were used, then each type's ATK, LET, DEF and HP.
  3. Skills: leader heroes, widgets and selected joiners. Displayed stats don't show every effect.
  4. Losses: which rows fell, what survived, and what has since reinforced.

Find it in game

The banner marks eligibility. Check the rally window to see which four contributions were selected.

What stays with your march?

Your gear, charms and research don't add another set of rally bonuses. Other heroes can increase deployment capacity, but their skills don't transfer. Neither do skills 2 and 3 of your first hero.

If you lack the called hero, ask whether to send troops without heroes. Sloth's rally explanation

Skill selection and stacking

Check which four skills the rally selected. An unwanted hero can take a slot; a fifth Gordon adds troops, but no fifth health contribution.

Duplicate Gordons stack. Duplicate Petras or Marlins don't give independent copies of their first-skill effect. A single Petra can still help when she isn't leading. Check each hero's interaction.

Joining the garrison

Leave lead accounts free for their assignments. Fill the castle with the best available troop tiers from the remaining joiners, using the called heroes and counts.

Check active bonuses when membership changes. The highest-stat member supplies them; the displayed captain may be someone else.

Keep your intended defender inside. An infantry refill means infantry, even if your saved preset is mixed.

Captain and bonus source

The displayed captain doesn't explain every defensive role. Inspect the active stats and skills, especially when players arrive or leave. Sloth's defensive roles

Build the army

Infantry buy time for cavalry and archers to deal damage. Too few expose those rows early; too many can leave you short of damage.

Use your lead's tested split for the heroes and opponent you're facing. Adjust from reports.

Gen 4: the 60 / 40 garrison

60% infantry, 40% cavalry, no archers. Margot and Alcar give those two troop types the damage and survival support to make this work.

  • Margot's third skill: cavalry have a 25% chance of an extra attack at 200% damage at skill 5.
  • Her defensive kit: squad-wide dodge chance and a defender-lethality widget support the hold.
  • Alcar: adds infantry damage and defensive support. The heroes make the formation.

Attack leads may answer cavalry-heavy holds with very low-cavalry formations. Follow their call for the actual heroes and opponent; offensive and garrison presets can differ sharply.

When the garrison is full

Surplus troops take spaces needed by another type. The lead must arrange recalls or replacements. Count reinforcements already travelling, and keep the key defender inside during incoming attacks.

Testing a different split
  1. Record both armies' troops, tiers, bonuses and heroes.
  2. Change one thing: split, leader hero or joiner mix.
  3. Compare several reports; chance skills vary.
  4. Check capture, survivors and healing cost. You still need to hold afterwards.
