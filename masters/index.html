Masters

Eight masters, each with an affinity ladder, a talent and four skills. Record yours to see what comes next and what your emblems and manuscripts can score.

{% include ascii/motif.html kind="tome" %}

Browse masters

Masters join the Master Academy in four waves, from server day 113. Affinity comes from Journey and gifts; every ten levels a new class costs that master's emblems and raises the talent. Skills unlock with affinity and are learned with Master's Manuscripts.

{% include masters/roster.html %}

KvK points from your masters

An emblem or manuscript only scores when it is spent. This counts what your masters can actually take: the next class for each master whose affinity bar is full, paid with its own emblems first and then general ones, and whole skill levels, cheapest first, once each level's requirement is met.

Special Research is not counted yet. It takes emblems from affinity 100, but the two sources disagree on its cost per research (25,000 or 14,000 emblems a master), so emblems kept for it are left out until one is confirmed in game.

Each kind counts on the KvK prep day that pays most for it. The Town Centre adds these to the rest of your bag.
