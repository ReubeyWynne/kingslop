      What's yours

      Many decorations only come from event packs. Mark the pieces you own (tap a piece, then set how many you have) and they come first wherever you pick one. Pieces you don't own stay in the plan, dimmed in the pickers, so you can plan ahead.

      Sources & confidence

      Piece art, sizes and limits, and the five game layouts, come from the KS Companion island catalogue (October 2026) and are not yet checked in game. Crazy golf, Harbour market, Deep woods and Royal gardens are our own designs. Which ground is buildable is still unknown, so the board only stops pieces overlapping. Path types over 500 tiles are flagged, never trimmed.
