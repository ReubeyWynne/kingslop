What's yours

Many decorations only come from event packs. Tap a piece and set how many you have.

Each pond costs 200 gems, once: a pond you buy stays yours, so 100,000 gems buys all 500.

Sources & confidence

Piece art, sizes and limits, and the five game layouts, come from the KS Companion island catalogue (October 2026) and are not yet checked in game. Crazy golf, Harbour market, Deep woods and Royal gardens are our own designs. Which ground is buildable is still unknown. Decorations (trees, hedges, fences and lamps) and ponds go up to 500 each, and event buildings are generally one each, or as many as their buff counts. Paths have no limit: the game's own layouts use up to 1,255 of one kind.
