What comes in each day

Nothing to show yet. A balance gets a rate once you have recorded it at least three times, three or more days apart from first to last.

A rate counts what came in between your readings over the last four weeks. What you spent is shown apart and never taken off as a loss, but spending between two readings hides income, so rates err low. Each goal is projected on its own from today, as if you spend nothing more on what it needs. Save-file imports and resets are not income.

Spend it when it scores

  1. 1

    Hold what scores.

    Truegold, Tempered Truegold, hero shards and widgets, master emblems, Forgehammers, governor materials, pet Advances and speedups all score in KvK prep and Strongest Governor. Unless an upgrade is blocking your Town Centre, do it on its scoring day.

  2. 2

    Never leave a queue idle.

    Holding materials is not holding time. Keep both builders and the Academy working on steps that cost no scoring materials, and start long ones with buffs on.

  3. 3

    Count before you hold.

    The Town Centre and Event Cycle count what your bag can score on each day, so you can see whether a step is worth waiting for.
