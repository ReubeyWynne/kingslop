# Governor gear artwork — provenance

Original Kingshot game artwork, published by Century Games and served by the
Kingshot Optimizer community tool. Retrieved 10 October 2026 from the public
Governor Gear and Charms pages' image references:
https://kingshotoptimizer.com/governor-gear and https://kingshotoptimizer.com/charms.
No generated artwork is included.

| Local files | Source URLs |
| --- | --- |
| `gear/{inf,cav,arc}-{1,2}-{tier}-{grade}.webp` | `https://kingshotoptimizer.com/images/governor-gear-cards/{infantry,cavalry,archery}_gear_{1,2}_{tier}_t{grade}_s0.webp` |
| `charms/{inf,cav,arc}-{1..22}.webp` | `https://kingshotoptimizer.com/images/charms-cards/{infantry,cavalry,archery}_lvl{1..22}.webp` |

Gear cards are downscaled from 384 to 160 pixels, charm cards to 72 pixels, then re-encoded as WebP. Resource icons are
unchanged. Each gear card is the zero-star version for its tier and grade; the
level text beside it shows the stars. The source's slot order matches the in-game
profile: infantry 1 is the armour and 2 the trousers, cavalry 1 the hat and 2
the amulet, archery 1 the ring and 2 the staff. Assets are served from this
repository, without runtime hotlinks. These files are game art, not the
reference site's interface or code.
