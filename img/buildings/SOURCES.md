# Building artwork — provenance

Original Kingshot game artwork, published by Century Games and served by the
Kingshot Optimizer community tool. Retrieved 17 October 2026 (the first nine) and
28 October 2026 (the rest) from the building icons its Buildings pages
reference: https://kingshotoptimizer.com/.
No generated raster artwork is included.
