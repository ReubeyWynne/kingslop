# Item artwork — provenance

Original Kingshot game artwork, published by Century Games and served by the
Kingshot Optimizer community tool. Retrieved 17 October 2026 from the resource
icons its pages reference: https://kingshotoptimizer.com/.
No generated artwork is included.

`hero-widget.webp` is cut from an in-game Backpack screenshot supplied by the
site owner on 17 October 2026 (Petra's Fate's Writ Widget). Widgets are hero
specific, so this stands for all of them. Its tile background and count were
removed, and the result is 96 pixels.

The other 64-pixel WebP files are unchanged. Assets are served from this
repository, without runtime hotlinks. No source had art for the healing
speedup, so that tile keeps its letter.
