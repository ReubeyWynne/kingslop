# Masters

The Masters page (`masters/`) lists the eight masters of the Master Academy, records yours in the shared ledger and counts the KvK points your emblems and manuscripts can score. The Town Centre's Masters shelf shows the same roster and adds those points to the event totals.

## Data

`_data/masters.json` is generated by `.dsh/build-masters.py` from the progression research (`research/progression/data/masters.json` in the project files). Do not edit it by hand. Each master has:

- `unlockDay`, `title`, `focus` (the Special Research troop: `infantry`, `cavalry`, `archery` or `all`) and `kingdom` (the generation or Truegold step that marks it upcoming on the kingdom timeline);
- `kindredSoul`, the buff at affinity 100;
- `affinity`: points to the next level and the class ladder (levels 1, 10, 20 … 100, each with its emblem cost), and `emblemsToMax`, their sum;
- `talent`: eleven levels, one per class;
- `skills`: four skills, each level with its effects, manuscripts, learning XP and requirement (an affinity level, or a total of the master's four skill levels).

`scope`, `summary` and `who` say in plain words what each master is for and who wants it, and each talent and skill carries `say` (one sentence at its top level, paraphrasing the in-game description with the data's values filled in), `ramp` (how each value grows from level 1) and, for skills, `where` (the event or system it applies to). They come from `.dsh/masters-guide.json`, which cites where each description was read; the build fails if a sentence names a value the top level lacks.

`sources` and `verified` record which databases list a master. Kingshot Optimizer and Kingshot Data agree row by row for Valora, Pan, Roman, Cassia, Guinevere and Wilson. Aena and Isnor are on Kingshot Optimizer only, so their cards say they rest on one source. Power is not carried.

Art in `img/masters/` is Kingshot game art; `img/masters/SOURCES.md` lists where each file came from.

## Spending plan

`js/master-spend.js` (`MasterSpend`) decides what the bag can be spent on, because event points are paid per piece consumed:

- **Emblems.** A class costs that master's emblems and can only be paid once the affinity bar is full at level 9, 19 … 99. Affinity points are not tracked, so the player marks the full bar (`ready`). Own emblems pay first; General Master Emblems are shared between waiting masters with `HeroSpend.allocate`, the grouped knapsack that shares general hero shards. A class is paid whole or not at all. Converting a general emblem scores nothing; spending it does.
- **Manuscripts.** Whole skill levels, cheapest first across every master, each once its requirement is met. A class the emblems pay counts first, so a skill it opens can take manuscripts in the same plan.
- **Unknowns.** No masters recorded, an unread affinity or skill level, or an uncounted balance count as nothing and are listed, so a total never runs high.

`MasterSpend.budget` hands the spendable counts to `EventRules.calculate`; `MasterSpend.points` scores one master on the best day for each item (Master Emblem × 6,000 on KvK prep and Strongest Governor, × 3,600 in Brawl; manuscripts × 60 and × 36). Learning speedups are ordinary speedups and count on rows that name learning or master.

**Special Research is not counted.** It takes emblems from affinity 100, but the sources disagree on its emblem cost per research (5 / 20 / 30 / 60 against 10 / 10 / 20 / 20 on four of its pages), so emblems kept for it are left out until one is confirmed in game. Adding it means a new emblem sink in `plan` once the cost is settled.

## Ledger

`masters.roster` and `master-emblems-{master}` balances; see the master section of `PLAYER-LEDGER.md`.

## Page

- `_includes/masters/roster.html` is the roster with each master's dossier and "Your master" form, shared by the Masters page and the Town Centre (`bag="off"` leaves out the emblem and manuscript bag). Masters are grouped by unlock day. Every dossier is a `details` with `data-deep-link="master-{id}"`, so `#master-valora` opens Valora's.
- `js/master-directory.js` paints the tiles and forms from the ledger and writes edits back.
- `js/master-points.js` paints the KvK points table.
- The Tome scene (`tome` in `.dsh/build-ascii.py`) is the page hero and deck cover.

## Checks

- `.dsh/master-spend-check.cjs`: data totals, art, the ledger section, the plan, Town Centre totals, navigation and dictionaries.
- `.dsh/masters-ui-check.cjs`: the page and the Town Centre shelf in a browser, every language at five widths, and the no-JavaScript fallback.
